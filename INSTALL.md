# Installing GP Banner Maker Tool (Windows)

1. Go to the **[Releases page](https://github.com/nicholu8/gp-banner-maker-tool/releases/latest)**.
2. Under **Assets**, download `GP-Banner-Maker-Tool-Setup-<version>.exe`.
3. Double-click the downloaded file.
4. Windows may show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** screen. This is normal for small free apps that
   are not code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
5. Click **Next** through the installer. It installs just for you and does not ask for an administrator password.
   Leave "Create a desktop shortcut" ticked if you want an icon on your desktop.
6. Click **Finish**. The app opens. Next time, use the desktop icon or the Start menu.

## Using it
Drop a photo on the window (or **Open photo…**), fill in the title, date, time, location and cost, and click
**Export PNG + PDF**. A green message confirms the files were saved. Use **Settings…** to change location colors
or add banner sizes.

## Updating
When a new version is out, the app shows a yellow "Version X is available" note. Click its link, download the new
installer and run it; it replaces the old version and keeps your settings. (You can turn the check off in
**Settings… > General**.)

## Uninstalling
Windows **Settings > Apps > Installed apps > GP Banner Maker Tool > Uninstall**. Your saved settings and exported
banners are not deleted.

## Can't run installers?
Download the `.zip` from the same Releases page, unzip it anywhere, and double-click `GPBannerMaker.exe` inside the
folder. Keep the folder together; the exe needs the files next to it.
