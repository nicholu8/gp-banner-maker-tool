# About GP Banner Maker Tool

**What it does:** you give it an event photo and type in the event details. It puts the Grow Pittsburgh banner
(the colored box with the title, date, time, location and cost) on the photo and saves it as pictures and files you
can post, email, or open in Canva.

You do not need to know anything technical. If you can drop a photo into a window and type in a few boxes, you can
use it.

## 1. Install it (one time)

You need a Windows computer.

1. Click this link to download the installer:
   **https://github.com/nicholu8/gp-banner-maker-tool/releases/latest/download/GP-Banner-Maker-Tool-Setup.exe**
2. When the download finishes, open it (in your browser's downloads list or your **Downloads** folder) by
   double-clicking **GP-Banner-Maker-Tool-Setup**.
3. Windows may show a blue box that says **"Windows protected your PC."** This is normal for small free programs.
   Click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.
4. Click **Next** a few times, then **Finish**. You do not need an administrator password.
5. The program opens. From now on, open it with the icon on your desktop, or search **GP Banner Maker Tool** in the
   Start menu.

**Updating:** when a newer version is available, a yellow note appears at the top of the program. Click its link,
download the new installer and run it the same way. Your settings are kept.

## 2. Make a banner

1. **Add the photo.** Drag a photo from your computer onto the big gray box, or click **Open photo…** and pick one.
   Phone photos (including iPhone photos) work too.
2. **Fill in the boxes on the right:**
   - **Title:** the name of the event.
   - **Date** and **Time:** type them however you want them to look, for example `Saturday, May 3` and `10am to 2pm`.
   - **Location:** choose a Grow Pittsburgh location from the list (this also picks the banner color), or type
     one in.
   - **Cost:** for example `Free` or `$10`.
3. **Look at the preview.** The banner updates as you type.
   - If your photo is tall, drag the photo up or down in the preview (or use the slider next to it) to choose which part
     shows.
   - If the title is long and splits onto two lines, you can tick **Keep title on one line** to force one line.
   - A small orange note appears under the boxes if something is missing (for example, no date).
4. **Click Export.** The button says what it will make, for example **Export 4 files**. A green message tells you
   when it is done.

**Banner colors** are chosen from the location, so you don't pick them yourself. A location the program doesn't know
yet gets the standard maroon. If you typed a new location you will use again, click **Save "…" to my locations**.

## 3. Where your files go

Each time you export, the program saves files in a folder on your computer. By default it is
**Documents > GrowPGH Banners**. You can change it with **Browse…** under "Output folder".
Click **Open folder** to see your files.

For an event called "Spring Plant Sale" you will get files like:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `spring-plant-sale.png` | The banner as a picture (wide, 1880 × 940). Good for websites and social media. |
| `spring-plant-sale.pdf` | The same banner as a PDF, for Canva (see below). |
| `spring-plant-sale-1080x1080.png` and `.pdf` | A square version, good for Instagram and Facebook. |

If you export the same title twice, the second set gets a number (`-2`) so nothing is overwritten.

## 4. Save to Google Drive (optional, one-time setup)

This copies every banner you make into the shared **GP Banners** folder, in a folder for the year and then a folder named after the event, so the team can find
them. Your files are always saved on your computer first.

**Before you start:** your **GP Banner Tool Admin** will give you access to the Grow Pittsburgh Shared Drive and send you
the **link to the GP Banners folder**. Keep that link handy.

**Set it up (once):**
1. In the program, click **Settings…**, then the **Google Drive** tab.
2. Click **Connect Google Drive**. Your web browser opens a Google sign-in page.
3. Sign in with your **Grow Pittsburgh** Google account and click **Allow**. The page then says you can close it.
4. Back in the program, the tab says "Connected as" and your email.
5. Paste the link to the **GP Banners** folder into the box, then click somewhere else. A green check and the folder name
   mean it worked.
6. Make sure **Upload to Drive after export** is ticked, then click **Save**.

**Opens the wrong browser?** If your computer opens a different browser than the one you use for your Grow Pittsburgh
account, use the link instead: click **Connect without opening a browser (copy link)**, then paste the link (it is
already copied) into the right browser's address bar and press Enter. Keep the Settings window open while you do it. You can also
click **Copy link** in the box that appears under the normal Connect button.

**After that**, every time you click Export the program also uploads the files. A message at the bottom says "Uploaded 4
files to Drive."

- If a file with the same name is already in the folder, the program asks: **Replace** it, **Keep both**, or **Skip**.
  Tick "Do this for the rest of this upload" to use the same answer for all the files.
- If the upload does not work (for example, no internet), your files are still saved on your computer, and a **Retry
  upload to Drive** button appears. Click it when you are back online.
- To stop using Drive, open Settings > Google Drive and click **Disconnect**.

The program only sends the banner files you export, and only after you connect.

## 5. Using the files

- **Posting a picture (website, Facebook, email):** use the `.png` file.
- **Canva:** in Canva choose **Create a design > Import file** and pick the `.pdf`. The text stays editable. Canva
  may show the lighter lines (date, location) a bit thin; select them and make them bold if you want.
- **Quick paste:** click **Copy PNG to clipboard**, then paste (Ctrl + V) straight into an email or document.

---

## 6. Settings

Click **Settings…** to change how the program behaves. Click **Save** when you are done.

- **Locations:** the list of locations and their banner colors. You can add a location, remove one, change its color,
  and choose white or black text. Locations not in the list use the "default" color shown at the bottom.
- **Sizes:** every size in this list is made each time you export. For each size you can tick **PNG**, **PDF**, or both
  (at least one stays ticked). Click **Add size** for a new shape (give it a name, width and height). The standard wide
  size cannot be changed or removed.
- **General:** turn the "check for new versions" notice on or off.
- **Google Drive:** see section 4.

## 7. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Windows says "Windows protected your PC" | Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This is expected. |
| "Can't open photo" | The file may not be a picture. Try a `.jpg` or `.png`. |
| A note says the photo "will look soft" | The photo is small. The banner still works, but a larger photo looks sharper. |
| The preview is empty | Add a photo first (drag it in or click **Open photo…**). |
| Google sign-in page says access is blocked | Make sure you are signed in with your **Grow Pittsburgh** account, not a personal Gmail. |
| "Can't open that folder" / "Google denied access" | You need access to the Shared Drive. Ask your GP Banner Tool Admin. |
| Upload failed | Check your internet and click **Retry upload to Drive**. Your files are safe on your computer. |
| "Your Google sign-in expired" | Open Settings > Google Drive, click **Disconnect**, then **Connect Google Drive** again. |
| Sign-in "timed out" | Click Connect again and finish signing in within 5 minutes. |
| Any other problem | Take a screenshot and send it to your GP Banner Tool Admin. |

**Uninstall:** Windows **Settings > Apps > Installed apps > GP Banner Maker Tool > Uninstall**. Your banners and
saved settings are not deleted.

**Can't run installers on your computer?** Download the `.zip` file from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/nicholu8/gp-banner-maker-tool/releases/latest), unzip it anywhere, and double-click
`GPBannerMaker.exe` inside the folder. Keep the folder together.

---

*Privacy:* the program has no accounts, no tracking, and sends nothing about you. It uses the internet only to check
GitHub for a newer version (you can turn this off in Settings) and, if you choose to connect it, to copy your exported
banner files to the Grow Pittsburgh Google Drive.
